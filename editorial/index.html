A note from Information Logistics

A note on operational drag.

Creative organizations rarely run short of ideas. They run short of uninterrupted attention: too many handoffs, too much coordination, too much practiced judgment spent shepherding routine work through systems that were never designed for it.

Information Logistics is our consultancy for that problem. We design and deliver AI pipelines and automation so machines can handle the routine and people can spend their time on judgment, taste, and original work.

The practice is end-to-end.

We map the work before choosing the technology. Then we build across the layers that need to change: AI pipeline design, agent systems, operations automation, enterprise AI governance, UX infrastructure, and design engineering. Strategy and implementation stay with one team.

“Machines handle the routine. Humans build what matters.”

Working principle
Information Logistics

That means the output is not a pitch deck describing an eventual system. It is the working surface: the orchestration, the tools, the interface, the evaluation loop, and a way for the team to operate what comes next.

The proof is both institutional and practical.

Our practice draws on twenty years of enterprise-platform experience and three US patents—US 10,430,504, US 9,967,332, and US 12,259,995. We also work directly in the current tooling. Monet, our open-source Claude Code harness, brings together 49 skills, 12 specialized agents, and 14 MCP tools.

That combination matters. Enterprise work teaches respect for governance, durability, and the people who inherit a system. Building in code keeps the advice honest.
