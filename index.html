What is forward-deployed design engineering?

We work inside the context of your teams and customers, with the people, systems, constraints, and evidence that shape the outcome.

What does Information Logistics do?

We design agent products and build the LLM agents inside them. The work can include product design, branding systems, marketing teams, deployment, and enablement.

When should a team bring Information Logistics in?

Bring us in when an agent, workflow, or product is blocked between functions; the problem is still unclear; or a prototype has no path into production.

What does an engagement leave behind?

Working software with the evaluation, controls, documentation, and workflows your team needs to operate and extend it.
